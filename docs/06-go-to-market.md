# HomeSwap India: Go-to-Market

- Seed 3 cities first (Mumbai, Goa, Bengaluru) with 300 homes each before opening bookings
- Founding-member pricing for the first 1,000 hosts
- Creator and travel-community partnerships; referral points
- Target second-home owners and remote workers first: they have idle homes and flexible dates

## Validation before building more
- Landing page with a waitlist; measure sign-up rate from a small paid or organic test.
- 20-50 customer interviews. Record the top 3 objections and address them in the product.
- Define a kill/continue threshold up front (for example, fewer than N waitlist sign-ups after 4 weeks means rethink).
