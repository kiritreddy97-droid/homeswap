# HomeSwap India: Business Plan

> Swap homes across Indian cities. Stop paying for hotels.

## Origin of the idea
Idea from a creator video: someone in Mumbai wants Goa, someone in Goa wants Mumbai; both pay for hotels. A points system makes swaps flexible; membership funds the platform.

## Problem
Hotels in popular cities (Goa, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi) cost thousands per night, while homes of travelling people sit empty. Travellers who want to visit each other's cities pay hotel rates in both directions.

## Solution
Verified members list their homes and earn points when others stay. They spend points to stay in any verified home in the network, so the two sides of a swap need not travel at the same time. This is exchange, not rental: no cash changes hands between members.

## Target users
- Leisure travellers (25-45) with a home in a metro or tourist city
- Remote workers who do month-long stays
- Families doing school-holiday trips
- Property owners with a second home that sits idle

## Revenue model
- Annual membership (core)
- Verification + damage-protection add-on
- Premium listing boosts and concierge swap-matching
- Partner commissions (cleaning, key handover, travel insurance)

## Key risks
- Trust and safety: property damage, theft, fake listings
- Liquidity: too few homes in a city makes the network useless
- Housing-society rules that forbid guests or short stays
- Regulatory classification as short-term rental
- Points inflation if listing bonus is too generous

## Success metrics
- Verified homes per city
- Swaps completed per month
- Points velocity (spent / earned)
- Membership renewal rate
- Incident rate per 1,000 stays
