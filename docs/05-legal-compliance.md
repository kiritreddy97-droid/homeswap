# HomeSwap India: Legal and Compliance Checklist

This is a working checklist, not legal advice. Have an Indian lawyer review it before launch.

- Treat as home exchange, not rental: document that no consideration passes between members
- Check housing-society bylaws; require host to confirm they are allowed to host guests
- KYC and background checks; store only what is needed (DPDP Act, 2023)
- Insurance partner for host damage protection; clear terms of liability
- GST on membership fees; state guest-registration/police intimation rules vary by state

## Always
- Privacy policy and terms of service before collecting any real user data.
- A grievance officer and a data-deletion path for users.
- Entity setup (e.g. private limited company), GST registration when thresholds apply.
