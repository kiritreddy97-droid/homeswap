HomeSwap India

Swap homes across Indian cities. Stop paying for hotels.

The problem

Hotels in popular cities (Goa, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi) cost thousands per night, while homes of travelling people sit empty. Travellers who want to visit each other's cities pay hotel rates in both directions.

How it works

Verified members list their homes and earn points when others stay. They spend points to stay in any verified home in the network, so the two sides of a swap need not travel at the same time. This is exchange, not rental: no cash changes hands between members.

Who it is for

  • Leisure travellers (25-45) with a home in a metro or tourist city
  • Remote workers who do month-long stays
  • Families doing school-holiday trips
  • Property owners with a second home that sits idle
